Start with the useful information

What to send, and what you get back

You do not need a formal engineering drawing. A clear sketch, photos, and the dimensions that control fit are often enough.

What to send

  • Your customer brief: forward the sketch, photos, dimensions, or existing file you already received.
  • Your print constraints: material, printer/process, preferred orientation, minimum wall, or shop-specific clearance preferences.
  • The required deliverables: STL/3MF only, STEP, or editable CAD as part of the job.
  • Any quoting deadline: tell us when you need a scope/price response so you can answer your customer.

What you get

  • Functional CAD support for brackets, adapters, holders, replacement parts, simple enclosures, and similar jobs.
  • STL / STEP modification when the customer already has a model that needs a practical change.
  • Print-ready handoff with the file types your shop needs for slicing, revision, or customer delivery.
  • A quiet partner workflow where your shop remains the customer-facing service unless you ask for something different.

FAQ

Questions people ask before sending a CAD job

Can our print shop outsource just one CAD job?

Yes. QuickPartCAD supports suitable individual overflow jobs without a retainer or subscription. Send the project details for a scope check and quote.

Can you work as a white-label CAD partner for our print shop?

Yes. By default, we can work through your shop: you collect the customer requirements, we handle the CAD, and you deliver/print the result. Direct customer communication is only needed if you want it.

Do we need an ongoing arrangement to start?

No. Start by sending one real job and we can quote that job first. If you later need recurring CAD support, the workflow can be discussed separately.

What kinds of jobs are a good fit?

Functional mechanical parts for 3D printing: brackets, adapters, holders, replacement parts, simple enclosures, mounts, and modest STL/STEP modifications.

What jobs are not a good fit?

Complex engineering programs, formal FEA/certification, advanced injection-molding engineering, and artistic or character sculpting are outside the focused QuickPartCAD offer.

Can we pass the files to our customer?

Yes, the normal workflow is to deliver the agreed CAD/print files back to your shop so you can use them for the customer order. If a specific licensing or ownership term matters for a larger project, state it in the brief so it can be agreed up front.

How do we start?

Send one real customer job with the available files, dimensions, and required output. We will tell you whether it is a quick edit, a rebuild, or a new CAD model and quote accordingly.
