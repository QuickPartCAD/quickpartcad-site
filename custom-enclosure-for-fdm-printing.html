Start with the useful information

What to send, and what you get back

You do not need a formal engineering drawing. A clear sketch, photos, and the dimensions that control fit are often enough.

What to send

  • Board or component dimensions: length, width, height, and any tall components.
  • Connector and control positions: USB, power, switches, buttons, displays, antennas, cable exits, and similar openings.
  • Mounting details: hole diameter and spacing, standoffs, screws, inserts, or preferred retention method.
  • Envelope and preferences: maximum outside size, wall thickness, lid direction, ventilation needs, and print material if known.

What you get

  • Enclosure CAD sized around your component envelope and mounting interfaces.
  • Base and lid/body parts with simple fastening or sliding/locating features as agreed.
  • Openings and mounting geometry for connectors, controls, cables, and standoffs.
  • STEP, STL, 3MF, and/or editable CAD for printing and later iteration.

FAQ

Questions people ask before sending a CAD job

Can you design an enclosure from PCB dimensions?

Yes. Provide the board outline, thickness, mounting-hole pattern, connector positions, and the maximum component heights. That is usually enough for a straightforward enclosure.

Can you work from a photo of the board?

A photo is useful for orientation, but a photo alone is not reliable for connector placement or mounting-hole positions. Add measured dimensions or a dimensioned drawing for the fit-critical features.

Can you add screw bosses or heat-set insert holes?

Yes. We can include simple bosses, through-holes, countersinks/counterbores, or insert locations when you provide the hardware size and preferred assembly method.

What clearance should I use for a 3D-printed lid?

There is no single universal value because printer calibration, material, orientation, and geometry matter. We can start with a practical FDM clearance and adjust it if you have a known printer/material preference.

Do you design the electronics too?

No. We design the mechanical enclosure around the dimensions and interface information you provide. PCB/electronics design is outside this focused service.
