Start with the useful information

What to send, and what you get back

You do not need a formal engineering drawing. A clear sketch, photos, and the dimensions that control fit are often enough.

What to send

  • Your current file: STL, STEP/STP, 3MF, or editable CAD if you have it.
  • A marked screenshot or short note: point to the exact feature that must change.
  • The target dimensions: hole diameter, spacing, thickness, offset, clearance, or other critical values.
  • Print context if relevant: material, nozzle/process, mating part, or fit requirement.

What you get

  • Updated geometry in the practical format for the job — usually STL, STEP, 3MF, and/or editable CAD.
  • A clean rebuild when needed if the original STL is too coarse or awkward to edit reliably.
  • Print-oriented fit for clearances, walls, holes, and mating features based on the dimensions you provide.
  • A clear handoff with the changed file(s), ready for your next print or slicing step.

FAQ

Questions people ask before sending a CAD job

Can you modify an existing STEP file?

Yes. STEP is usually the easiest starting point for a clean mechanical edit. Send the file plus the dimensions or marked change you need.

Can you modify an STL file?

Often, yes. Simple mesh changes can be practical. If the STL is very coarse, damaged, or the requested change needs clean parametric geometry, we may rebuild the simple part instead of forcing a poor mesh edit.

Can you convert an STL to STEP?

For simple functional parts, we can rebuild the geometry into clean CAD and deliver STEP. We do not describe this as an automatic one-click conversion: the goal is usable geometry, not just a different file extension.

How much does a simple modification cost?

Simple CAD edits start from $20. The actual quote depends on the file quality, number of changes, and whether a rebuild is needed.

What should I send for a quote?

Send the STL or STEP, a screenshot showing the change, and the target dimensions. That is usually enough to tell whether the job is a quick edit or a rebuild.
